A softphone for call-center agents

Connectix calls through the PBX you already have. It keeps your call history, can transcribe and summarise calls, and has a chat with a bot that answers questions about your calls. In English and Hebrew.

Download Contact us

What it does

Calls

Dial, answer, hold, transfer and take a second call, on your own SIP account. It shows when the line is ready and says plainly why when it is not.

Transcripts

When switched on, each call becomes text with a short summary. A speech model on your own computer can do it, so the audio need not leave your network. Only the text is kept.

Chat with a bot

Ask about today's calls, missed calls or a customer, and get the answer with a chart. A person can take over the conversation at any time.
