About Us

Who are we?

Connectix is developed in Israel by people who have built and run VoIP platforms for telecom companies for years: switches, SIP trunks, WebRTC softphones, call centers.

Connectix comes out of that work. It is a phone for the agentic workspace, built on Elixir and the BEAM, with bots that read the switch's events, hear a call, look up the customer, and hand the work to a person with a summary. Anything that changes something waits for a person's approval.

It runs on your hardware, next to your switch or on an operator's PC. The code is one Elixir application, tested end to end against a test PBX.

Connectix has no reference customers yet. The logos on the front page are companies we worked with on earlier platform work.

We are dedicated to better communication.
