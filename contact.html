If you are a VoIP provider, ITSP or carrier thinking about a pilot, it helps to tell us:

  • which switch you run, and whether it is FreeSWITCH;
  • which of the three jobs you have in mind: tier-1 support, NOC watch or provisioning;
  • roughly how many calls a day that line takes, and in which languages;
  • where Connectix would run: on your servers, or on an operator's PC.

We answer in plain words, with what a pilot would take.

The Windows desktop app

Each version is published on GitHub Releases: the installer, Connectix_<version>_x64-setup.exe, with a SHA256SUMS.txt to check it against.

Install it, open Connectix, sign in and add your SIP account in the gear. The phone shows Ready once your PBX has accepted the account.

The installer is not yet code-signed; Windows shows an unknown-publisher warning on first run.
